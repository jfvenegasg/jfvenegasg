## About me

I am an Industrial Civil Engineer, university lecturer and PhD researcher in Engineering Sciences, focused on building technology for real-world decision-making.

My current work is centered on **territorial intelligence**, **urban metabolism**, **circular economy**, **waste management**, **CO₂e mitigation**, **data products**, and **AI-enabled sustainability solutions**.

I move between research, product strategy and technical execution: from mathematical modeling and data pipelines to business validation, pilots, dashboards and scalable digital products.
